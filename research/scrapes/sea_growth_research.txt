## SEA FM Market Data
Source: Frost & Sullivan / ResearchAndMarkets via web search (September 2025 reports)
- SEA FM market: USD 15.34B (2024) → USD 22.59B (2030), CAGR 6.7–8.5%
- Mature market: Singapore
- High-growth markets: Indonesia, Vietnam, Philippines
- Key driver: "First-time outsourcers" — companies in developing SEA shifting from in-house to outsourced FM
- Outsourcing trend: expanding asset bases, evolving FM needs, sustainability requirements
- Green building: Singapore Green Mark, LEED (Vietnam/Thailand), Estidama driving new contracts
- ESG: Procurement teams evaluating ESG reporting capability when selecting FM vendors
- Challenge: High preference for in-house FM teams in certain markets/sectors. Unregulated market.
- FM consolidation happening in SEA (parallel to GCC trend)

## SEA B2B Growth Challenges
Source: Firmable B2B Outbound Guide 2026; ECXO Asia B2B CX Benchmark 2025
URL: https://firmable.com/sg/resources/blog/how-to-run-b2b-outbound-in-southeast-asia/
1. Market fragmentation: SEA = 10+ distinct markets with different buying behaviors, decision structures
2. Hierarchy: Outside Singapore, final decision sits with most senior stakeholder — top-down approach works best
3. Trust gap: Case studies from US/Europe don't convert. Local references 3x more effective.
4. Spread too thin: Companies that try to "cover SEA from day one" create shallow pipeline across multiple countries
5. 12-18 month learning curve for new market entry
6. Partner quality: Rushed partner selection leads to poor-fit distributors
7. Relationship-led: Who you've worked with matters before what you offer
8. Decision-making: Outside Singapore, buying process is more hierarchy-driven and relationship-based

## B2B Creative Growth in SEA
Source: Hubspot APAC Sales in Asia Guide; Callbox; ARC Group
URL: https://www.hubspot.com/web-guide/apac/resources/sales-in-asia/partners
- Local partners: deeply understand the region, have built-in brand trust, own customer relationships
- Depth in one market > shallow coverage across four
- Indonesia: national coverage through sub-distributor networks (archipelago problem)
- Thailand: modern trade networks, established retail relationships required
- KOL/trusted advisor model: recommendation-based selling 3x more effective than direct
- Relationship-led: trust before transaction in most SEA markets
